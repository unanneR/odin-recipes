Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Description

While not traditionally South African, this is a beloved comfort food globally. it consists of sliced chicken breast and pasta (usually fettuccine) tossed in a rich, creamy sauce made from butter, heavy cream, and parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz Fettuccine pasta
  • 2 Chicken breasts (sliced)
  • 2 cups Heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 1 cup Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves Garlic (minced)

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Season and sear chicken in a pan until cooked through; set aside.
  3. In the same pan, melt butter and sauté garlic for 30 seconds. Pour in the heavy cream and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Whisk in the Parmesan cheese until the sauce thickens.
  5. Toss the pasta and chicken into the sauce. Season with black pepper and parsley.
