Bobotie

Description

Often considered the national dish of South Africa, Bobotie is a fragrant, spiced minced meat bake topped with a savory egg custard. It’s a perfect mix of sweet (apricots/raisins) and savory (curry spices).

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Ground beef or lamb
  • 2 Onions (chopped)
  • 2 slices White bread (soaked in milk)
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder, 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 3 tbsp Fruit chutney (Mrs. Ball’s is traditional)
  • 1/4 cup Raisins or sultanas
  • Topping: 2 Eggs, 1 cup Milk, 4 Bay leaves.

Steps

  1. Sauté onions, garlic, and ginger. Add curry powder and cook until fragrant.
  2. Add the meat and brown. Stir in tomatoes and potatoes.
  3. Simmer until the meat is tender and the potatoes have thickened the sauce.
  4. Cut the bread loaf in half or quarters. Scoop out the middle (the "virgin"), leaving a thick crust wall.
  5. Ladle the curry into the bread hollow and place the scooped-out bread on top for dipping.
