Bunny Chow

Description

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf White bread (unsliced)
  • 1 lb Lamb or chicken (cubed)
  • 2 Potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 Onion, 2 cloves Garlic, 1 tsp Ginger (minced)
  • 3 tbsp Durban curry powder
  • 1 can Chopped tomatoes

Steps

  1. Sauté onions, garlic, and ginger. Add curry powder and cook until fragrant.
  2. Add the meat and brown. Stir in tomatoes and potatoes.
  3. Simmer until the meat is tender and the potatoes have thickened the sauce.
  4. Cut the bread loaf in half or quarters. Scoop out the middle (the "virgin"), leaving a thick crust wall.
  5. Ladle the curry into the bread hollow and place the scooped-out bread on top for dipping.
